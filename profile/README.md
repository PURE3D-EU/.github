## Bringing 3D into the scholarly record








**PURE3D is a research infrastructure for publishing, preserving and exploring 3D worlds and objects.** We bring 3D models together with the context, interpretation and documentation that make them useful for research, teaching and cultural heritage.








A scholarly 3D edition goes beyond a model: textual and multimedia annotations explain what an object represents, how it was created and the decisions behind its interpretation.








### Publish. Preserve. Explore.








| Publish | Preserve | Explore |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Present 3D scholarship through editions with contextual annotations and multimedia. | Support long-term access and the FAIR principles: Findable, Accessible, Interoperable and Reusable. | Discover cultural heritage objects and environments through interactive 3D viewing. |
